EXPERIMENT 2: Targets that are not phase-type, fitted with p phases (dtype 'general', the default)
KS: the largest distance between the fitted and the target CDF. Mean: relative error of the
fitted mean, which the EM fixed point makes equal to the target mean. Var: fitted/target
variance. q95: relative error of the 95% quantile. SCV: fitted variance/mean^2, which is at
least 1/p for any PH distribution. KS 1e-7: KS with the tolerance tightened from the default
1e-5 to 1e-7; where it is smaller, the default stopped the slow EM before the optimum.
